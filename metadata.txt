about=Urban Procedural 3D Studio links a plan's rules to the buildings they allow. Select parcel or block boundaries in QGIS and open them in a local Three.js editor. Change setbacks, heights and typologies and the massing rebuilds at once, with the coverage, floor-area and height figures updated beside it so you see whether the scheme still complies.
    When the design is right, send it back: the generated buildings and their compliance metrics land in your QGIS project as ordinary layers, ready for maps, tables and further analysis.
    The editor runs on a local server; your data stays on your computer.
    Manual: https://geophilo.com/planx_urban_procedural_3d/manual.html
